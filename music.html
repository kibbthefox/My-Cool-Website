The Road Trilogy

I released a new project recently.

Here is a little preview into the albums:

The Road Trilogy is a project that has been in the works for over a year.
The idea started with Not a Road and branched out to A Road and Down the Road, each album
communicating and adding to an overall narrative about conformity. A Road is a statement of
what I am/have been. Not a Road is trying to stay in the bounds of what is normal, succeeding
and failing in various ways. Down the Road lets loose and is by far my most experimental
album so far, truly showing identity and having fun while doing it. Beyond this theme, each

album has its own symbols and ideas that it communicates independently of the trio.
